## 2. Weekly report (#278, #282)
- Segment control «تقرير الأسبوع» / «الوزن» / «الإنجازات» (the third shows «قريبًا» until K.6b). Header range «18 – 24 أكتوبر» (Latin digits, as in K.4), arrows for previous weeks back to 12 weeks, next disabled on the current week. A week = the 7 days ending today (or ending on the chosen week's last day).
- Hero ring: adherence = days with status ACHIEVED ÷ days with a non-null status in the week. Text by result: ≥ 0.8 «أسبوع ممتاز!», ≥ 0.5 «أسبوع جيد», otherwise «كل يوم فرصة جديدة»; sub-line «حققت هدفك في N من M أيام» (ICU gender select). Days with `log_count` 0 are «لا تسجيل» and excluded from M.
- Three cards: «أفضل يوم» = the ACHIEVED day whose `totals.kcal` is closest to `target_kcal` (show weekday and completion as a percent of target from server numbers); «أكثر ما نقصك» = the nutrient that appears most often in `unmet_minimums`, with the average server `total` vs `minimum` on those days; «متوسط الماء» = average daily confirmed water from the local `water_logs` over days that have any water log (ml a day). Hide a card when it has no data.
- Calories chart: 7 bars of `totals.kcal` against a dashed `target_kcal` line; bar color by server status (ACHIEVED green, PARTIAL amber, NOT_ACHIEVED red-muted, null gray); weekday labels follow RTL order; legend «ضمن الهدف» / «قريب» / «بعيد» / «لا تسجيل».
- Macros card «متوسط العناصر»: average protein, carbs, fat in grams over logged days vs the current target grams (`GET /me/targets`), three bars. No percentages of energy.
- Exceeded limits: if any day has `exceeded_limits`, a small card «تجاوزت حدًا صحيًا في N أيام» listing the nutrient names (sodium, sugars, saturated fat) with the server `limit` and `total`; wellness tone, no medical claim.
- Empty week (no logs): illustration and «سجّل وجباتك هذا الأسبوع لترى تقريرك هنا» with a button to Today. General mode (no plan): show the calories chart with gray bars and no target line if `target_kcal` is null, and hide the ring.
