## Why
Decision #88 says "kcal within ±10% on every **feasible** day". For cpsat, `kcal_relaxed=True` happens only when phase 1 (all hard limits + the ±10% kcal band) returned INFEASIBLE, which is a solver proof that no plan within the band exists under those hard limits and that day's previous-day exclusions. Such a day is not a feasible day in the #88 sense. Greedy gives no such proof, so greedy days keep the plain PASS/FAIL. This is Decision #121.
