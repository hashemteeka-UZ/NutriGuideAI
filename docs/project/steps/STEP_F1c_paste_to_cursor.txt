## 1. `app/services/condition_limits.py` — resolution (§28.2)

`resolve_condition_limits(session, user_id, target_kcal) -> ResolvedLimits` (frozen dataclass):
- Collect `condition_nutrient_limits` rows for all of the user's conditions (`user_health_conditions`). A user with no condition gets an empty result (no limits, §30.1 known limitation #69).
- Per nutrient, per kind:
  - **max_per_day**: convert `PERCENT_ENERGY` to grams with the user's `target_kcal` (fat and saturated fat 9 kcal/g; carbohydrate, sugars, protein 4 kcal/g), then take the **minimum** of all maxima (strictest wins).
  - **min_per_day**: the **maximum** of all minima (same conversion).
  - **max_per_meal**: keep `ABSOLUTE` maxima as amounts (minimum wins); keep `PERCENT_ENERGY` per-meal maxima as a percentage of **that meal's own energy** (§9.5b), minimum percentage wins. Do not convert per-meal percentages with the daily target.
- Keep, for every resolved value, the list of `(condition_code, source_reference)` it came from (needed for the snapshot and the report).
- **Conflict**: if for any nutrient effective min_per_day > effective max_per_day, raise `ConditionConflictError` naming the nutrient, both values and the conditions involved. Never relax a limit.
- Tag restrictions (`condition_tag_restrictions`): `avoid_tags` = union of `AVOID` tag codes; `limit_tags` = for each `LIMIT` tag, the smallest non-null `max_servings_per_week` (a `LIMIT` row with NULL contributes no number; if all rows for that tag are NULL, record the tag with `None`).
- Provide `to_snapshot() -> dict` (JSON-serialisable: numbers as strings of the exact Decimal, never floats).

## 2. `app/services/meal_filter.py` — Layer 1 (§28.1, §9.4b)

`SLOT_OCCASION = {"BREAKFAST": "breakfast_suitable", "LUNCH": "lunch_suitable", "DINNER": "dinner_suitable", "SNACK": "snack_suitable"}` (decision: `beverage` and `nuts_seeds_snack` alone do not make a meal a snack candidate; every slice snack also carries `snack_suitable`).

`evaluate_meals(session, user_id, slot, limits: ResolvedLimits, servings_multiplier=Decimal("1.0")) -> list[MealEvaluation]` returns **every** meal (active or not) with `eligible: bool` and `exclusion_codes: list[str]`. A meal is excluded if any rule is true; collect **all** failing rules, not only the first:
1. `INACTIVE` (`is_active = false`) or `INCOMPLETE_NUTRIENTS` (`is_meal_nutritionally_complete` is false).
2. `ALLERGEN:<allergen name_en>` for each `meal_allergens` row matching `user_allergen_prefs` (any severity).
3. `EXCLUDED_INGREDIENT:<ingredient_id>` for each `meal_ingredients.ingredient_id` with stance `EXCLUDE` in `user_ingredient_prefs`. `DISLIKE` and `LIKE` never exclude.
4. `AVOID_TAG:<tag code>` for each meal tag (MANUAL or DERIVED) in `limits.avoid_tags`.
5. `MAX_PER_MEAL:<nutrient code>` when the amount for `servings_multiplier` servings (`amount_per_serving × multiplier`) exceeds a resolved absolute `max_per_meal`, or its energy share exceeds a resolved percentage `max_per_meal` (kcal factors as in §1).
6. `NO_OCCASION:<slot>` when the meal lacks the slot's occasion tag.

`eligible_meal_ids(...)` is a thin helper over the same function. The exclusion codes are for tests and the report; they are not stored.

## 3. `app/services/planner.py` — greedy one-day plan (assistant decisions for F.1)

`PLANNER_VERSION = "greedy_v1"`. Deterministic: same inputs, same plan.

Constants (one block, comment "Step F.1 planner decision, to be recorded in the next doc version"):
- Slot energy shares of `target_kcal`: `BREAKFAST 0.25`, `LUNCH 0.35`, `DINNER 0.25`; the remaining 0.15 is for snacks.
- `MULTIPLIERS = (0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0)` (§11.8).
- `MAX_SNACKS = 2`; snacks are added while day energy < `0.90 × target_kcal`, and never past `1.10 × target_kcal`.

`build_day_plan(session, user_id, limits, target, plan_date) -> DayPlan` (no writes):
1. Main slots in order BREAKFAST, LUNCH, DINNER. For each slot, consider every pair (eligible meal for that slot at that multiplier, multiplier). Pair rules: re-run rule 5 at that multiplier; a meal already used in this day is not allowed, and neither is a meal whose `variant_group` (§10.1, #76) is already used in this day; adding the pair must not push any running daily total above a resolved `max_per_day`; **lookahead**: running total + pair + the sum, over the main slots still to fill, of the smallest amount any eligible meal for that slot could add (at multiplier 0.5) must also stay within every `max_per_day`; the count of servings (sum of multipliers) of each `limit_tags` tag in the day must not exceed its weekly number (a one-day plan only counts its own day; weekly accounting across plans is out of F.1 scope, note it in the docstring).
2. Choose the pair minimising `|pair_kcal − slot_target_kcal|`; ties broken by smaller `|pair_protein − slot_share × target_protein_g|`, then `meals.ref_external`, then smaller multiplier.
3. If a main slot has no valid pair, raise `NoFeasiblePlanError(slot, reasons)` where `reasons` summarises how many candidates each rule removed. Never relax a limit and never drop a main slot.
4. Snacks: while fewer than `MAX_SNACKS` snacks and day energy < 0.90 × target, add the SNACK pair (same rules, no repeated meal or `variant_group` within the day) minimising `|remaining_gap − pair_kcal|` with day energy staying ≤ 1.10 × target; stop when no pair qualifies.
5. Result: items (slot, meal_id, multiplier, per-item nutrient amounts), day totals for every nutrient, `kcal_within_10pct: bool`, and `unmet_minimums` (each resolved `min_per_day` the day does not reach, with the gap). Energy precision and minimums are **soft** in greedy_v1: they are reported, not enforced. Every `max` rule and every Layer-1 rule is **hard**.

`reason_codes` per item (§28.6), deterministic, thresholds as constants:
- `FITS_KCAL_TARGET`: item energy within ±15% of its slot target (snacks: of the snack share split evenly by the number of snacks).
- `HIGH_PROTEIN`: protein energy (4 kcal/g) ≥ 20% of item energy.
- `LOW_SODIUM`: sodium ≤ 120 mg per 100 g; `LOW_SUGAR`: sugars ≤ 5 g per 100 g; `LOW_SATURATED_FAT`: saturated fat ≤ 1.5 g per 100 g (UK FSA "low" per 100 g); `HIGH_FIBER`: fiber ≥ 6 g per 100 g (EU "high fibre" claim). Use `meal_nutrients.amount_per_100g`.
- `SUITS_CONDITION_<CODE>`: one per user condition when the item respects every per-meal limit of that condition and carries none of its AVOID or LIMIT tags.
- `MATCHES_LIKED_INGREDIENT`: the meal contains an ingredient with stance `LIKE`.
- `NEW_FOR_VARIETY` is not emitted in F.1 (needs history).
Codes are stored sorted, as a JSON list of strings.

`create_day_plan(session, user_id, plan_date, now) -> MealPlan` (writes):
- `screen_user` must be eligible (raise otherwise); a current `user_targets` row must exist (raise otherwise); `resolve_condition_limits` with its `target_kcal` (conflict raises, no plan written).
- Insert `meal_plans` with `date_from = date_to = plan_date`, `generated_by = "greedy"`, `algorithm_version = "greedy_v1"`, `target_id` = current target, and `target_snapshot` = `{"formula_version", "target": {kcal, protein_g, carb_g, fat_g, fiber_g}, "limits": ResolvedLimits.to_snapshot(), "tag_rules_version": "tags_v1", "planner_version": "greedy_v1", "computation_version": "calc_v1"}` (exact Decimal strings). Insert one `meal_plan_items` row per item (`day_index = 0`, `was_swapped = false`, `reason_codes`).
- Never delete or update an older plan covering the same day (§11.7 current-plan rule #72). A second call creates a second plan; the newest is current.
